    How to read this

    Residual value
    What the asset is worth at the end of the term. Owning means you keep it, which lowers the true cost of buying. For real estate it grows with the appreciation rate.
    NPV (net present value)
    A dollar (or peso) paid in three years hurts less than one paid today. NPV converts every future payment into today's money using your discount rate, so the two options can be compared fairly. The option with the lower NPV of cost is cheaper.
    Implied rate
    The interest rate hidden inside the lease. It is the rate at which the lease payments (plus the asset's value at the end) exactly pay back the price. Compare it with your loan APR: if it's lower, the lessor is financing you more cheaply than your lender.
    After tax
    Lease payments are treated as a deductible expense. When you buy, you deduct loan interest and depreciation instead (land is never depreciated), and pay tax on any gain if the asset is worth more than its tax book value at the end.
