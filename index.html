How to read this

Residual value
What the equipment is worth at the end of the term. Owning means you keep it, which lowers the true cost of buying.
NPV (net present value)
A dollar paid in three years hurts less than a dollar paid today. NPV converts every future payment into today's dollars using your discount rate, so the two options can be compared fairly. The option with the lower NPV of cost is cheaper.
Implied rate
The interest rate hidden inside the lease. It is the rate at which the lease payments (plus the equipment's value at the end) exactly pay back the equipment price. Compare it with your loan APR: if it's lower, the lessor is financing you more cheaply than your lender.
After tax
Lease payments are treated as a deductible expense. When you buy, you deduct loan interest and straight-line depreciation instead, and pay tax on any gain if the equipment is worth more than its tax book value at the end.
